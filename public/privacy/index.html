Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · JoinThePOD LLC, operating Keep. Give. Live.

Inspire by KGL does not ask who you are. There is no account, no sign-in and no profile.

What Inspire receives

When you use Inspire inside an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude, the assistant sends our server the details of each request: a date, a theme, the words you searched for, a line id or a shade. We use them only to answer that request. We do not store them in a database.

Hosting logs

Our host, Netlify, keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address, time and the address requested) to run and protect the service. These are kept for a limited time under Netlify's own policies.

Fonts

The card loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your device contacts Google when a card is shown.

The assistant you use

Your conversation itself is handled by the assistant's provider under their privacy policy, not ours.

The shop

The Live button opens keepgivelive.com. Anything you do there, including an order, is covered by the shop's own privacy policy.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not show ads or track you across sites.
  • We do not use cookies in Inspire.

Contact

Questions or requests: yinde@keepgivelive.com. If this policy changes, we update the date above.
